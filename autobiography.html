// MY STORY

MY Autobiography From Tungie, Kenema — To The Whole Digital World

// CHAPTER 1 — The Beginning.

Hey there! hehe, I'm so glad telling you my full story lines, let's get started.
My name is Mustapha Gbanie. I was born on 20th May 2004, in Tungie Gorama Mende Chiefdom, into a loving middle-class family. My mother is Jeneba Gbanie, and my father is Kekura M Gbanie. I grew up and went through all my schooling — from primary level up to WASSCE — here in Kenema District, Sierra Leone.

From early on, I had a clear goal: I wanted to change my life and move my family from middle class to a higher, better standard of living. That focus made me choose to stay away from dating or romantic relationships nah hahaha! You know what? I knew those things could distract me or bring early responsibilities I wasn’t ready for. My biggest fear was taking on duties I couldn’t handle yet, and adding stress to my life when my family was still supporting me, hahaha . So I put all my attention into learning and building my future, trusting that hard work would lead me where I wanted to go one day.


CHAPTER 2 — Discovering Technology

As I watched the world grow and change with modern technology, I realized: technology is the path to big success. That was when I decided to dive deep into this field. I started attending computer classes at CG-DAT (Central Global Development for Advance Technology), and when I sat my exams for Introduction to Windows and Microsoft Word, I got excellent grades — that result gave me so much confidence and made me want to learn even more. huh!

I planned to go further into PowerPoint and other programs, but at that time, my father encouraged me to go to university. I began my first year at Eastern Technical University of Sierra Leone, studying BSc Social Work. After one year, my father asked me to change my course. I wanted to study ICT — because my heart was already fully in technology — but the fees were very high nah. I worried it would be too hard for my father to afford, but he told me: “Just go, Allah is the one who provides everything.” Koi bat nahi (nothing much).


CHAPTER 3 — Learning With Limited Resources

Even before university, I already knew exactly what I wanted to be: a Web Developer and even more than that. I started learning HTML fundamentals, and soon moved to CSS — I understood it very well, and loved every part of it. But there was one big challenge: I code entirely on my phone (Itel A56) — I have never used a computer or laptop until now, hahaha 😆, coincidence right?

My phone is old, slow, and the internet connection is often weak, which even paused my learning of JavaScript and PHP for a while. But I never gave up innit I kept going, learning every chance I got. At university, I realized the school didn’t offer the courses I really wanted, and there were no proper tools or computers to use. I found myself learning far more from online tutorials and my own research at home than I did in class. Still, I respected my father’s wish and kept attending classes — but whenever I had free time on campus, or late at night when everyone else was sleeping, I was busy coding and studying. That is how I mastered HTML and CSS, even with all the difficulties.

Today, I am proud of how far I have come. I am already visible on Google Search, bro — a big dream come true — and I am now starting my own small business: building websites for shop owners and businesses around me, but not too serious since I'm using local tools and building portfolios, helping them grow and be known online too.

My journey hasn’t been easy — no laptop, slow phone, limited resources — but my passion is stronger than any challenge. My goal is simple but big: to become a great developer, create things that will help people all over the world, build a name that is known everywhere, and leave a legacy that will never fade.
I am Mustapha — always learning, always building, always moving forward.

Aww! Thanks for listening my whole story lines, don't forget to get in touch for collaboration and new opportunity.
